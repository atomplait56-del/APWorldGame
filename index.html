Chronicle Depths

Doubts, Distortions, and Myths have overrun the archive beneath the world. Guide the cat wizard through gauntlets of unit-themed enemies to reach six challenges where historical thinking is your magic: restore timelines, trigger chain reactions of cause and effect, race to compare societies, reflect myths with real evidence, and defeat each unit's boss by building an argument mid-battle. Swing your staff, spend mana wisely, and call in TP to pull enemies off you. Every correct answer grants a protective ward and restores mana.
